FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kind of businesses do you work with?
Companies of any size, anywhere in the world, that have repetitive operations, high volumes of conversations or data, or a product that needs AI built in. If there's a process people do by hand every day, it's a candidate.
Do you build on OpenAI, Anthropic or open-source models?
All of them. We're model-agnostic and choose per task based on quality, latency, cost and data-residency needs. Systems are designed so models can be swapped without a rebuild.
How is pricing structured?
A one-time setup fee for discovery, design and build, then an optional retainer for managed operations. Infrastructure is billed transparently. Payment is milestone-gated: you approve and pay per phase. The discovery audit is free.
Who owns what you build?
You do. The code, prompts, business logic and documentation are yours. There's no proprietary lock-in, and we can hand everything over to your team at any point.
How do you keep our data secure?
Least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, and deployment in your cloud when required. AI actions are logged and scoped, and sensitive steps keep a human in the loop.
Can we white-label figuredoutai.tech?
Yes. The platform is built for white-labelling, so agencies and resellers can offer it under their own brand. Join the partnership list and we'll reach out ahead of launch.
